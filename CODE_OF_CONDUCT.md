# Community Code of Conduct

We want participation in iSpotify to be open, respectful, and useful.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, accept constructive feedback,
and give others room to learn. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing,
spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct
problems privately through the repository's security advisory form.
